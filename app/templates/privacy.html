Privacy Policy

SleepyBear LLC — Last updated: February 9, 2026

1. Introduction

SleepyBear LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates the WorkTracker application for internal workforce management. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information from our employees and contractors ("you").

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, and phone number provided during onboarding.
  • Work Data: Time entries, job assignments, hours worked, mileage, and pay information.
  • SMS Data: Phone numbers used for job assignment notifications. We do not store the content of SMS messages beyond delivery status.
  • Account Data: Login credentials (passwords are stored in hashed form only).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage job assignments and track work hours.
  • To send SMS notifications about job assignments and schedule updates.
  • To calculate payroll and generate reports.
  • To communicate with you about your work.

4. SMS Messaging

  • We send SMS messages to notify technicians of job assignments and schedule information.
  • Message frequency varies based on job assignments.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • For help with SMS, reply HELP or contact us at the information below.
  • We do not sell, share, or use phone numbers or SMS data for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • Phone numbers are only used for job-related notifications within our workforce management system.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data may be shared only:

  • With SMS service providers solely for message delivery.
  • As required by law or legal process.

Mobile contact information will not be shared with any third party or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Any sections of this Privacy Policy discussing information sharing exclude messaging opt-in and consent data — that information will not be shared with any third parties.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, JWT authentication, and role-based access controls to protect your information.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for the duration of your employment or contract with us, and as required by applicable labor and tax laws.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. Employees and contractors may also view and update their information through the WorkTracker application.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
